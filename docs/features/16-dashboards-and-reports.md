# Feature 16: Dashboards, Analytics and Reports

## Purpose
Give each role a useful view of applications, pending tasks and validation outcomes without exposing records outside its access scope.

## Institution dashboard
Application status, checklist completion, outstanding institution actions, clarification requests, recently submitted documents and upcoming permitted deadlines.

## Officer dashboard
Applications under review, open findings by type/severity, pending source verification, overdue tasks, institution responses awaiting review and processing-ageing summaries.

## Administration dashboard
Connector health, failed processing jobs, notification delivery, active rule versions and access/configuration events.

## Search and filters
Application ID, institution identifier/name, category, academic year, status, finding type, assigned reviewer and date range, subject to permissions.

## Reports
Missing/expired document analysis, findings and resolution time, source-verification outcome rates, case ageing, false-positive rates and measured extraction/validation performance.

## Data integrity
All counts come from database queries and must disclose their definitions and time windows. Avoid arbitrary risk scores without a documented method. Report measured results, not fabricated prototype accuracy. Export operations should enforce permissions and be audit logged.
